I'm a second year PhD student at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), working with Prof. [Sanidhya Kashyap](https://sanidhya.github.io/). Before that, I was a research intern in the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington in Seattle, advised by Prof. [Baris Kasikci](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~baris/) and Prof. [Yigong Hu](https://yigonghu.github.io/).

My current research focuses on **agentic RL training systems**, with an emphasis on efficient resource management and scheduling. Specifically, I explore new **systems principles and abstractions for large-scale agentic RL training of MLLMs and VLAs**, with the goal of improving end-to-end performance without compromising model convergence.
